## Every finding. One queue. Your approval required.

![](/uploads/illustrations/cuate/entries.svg)

Everything Canvas discovers, computes, or prepares surfaces in your Workflow Command Center as a structured item — alert, draft journal entry, draft email, checklist task, or advisory note. Each item carries full context: the data that triggered it, the recommended action, and the impact if unaddressed.

Your team reviews, approves or modifies, and publishes. Canvas logs every action with a full audit trail.

- **Alerts** — Issues requiring attention with source data and recommended action
- **Draft journal entries** — Prepared and balanced, waiting for your review and posting
- **Draft emails** — Client or vendor communications composed and ready to send
- **Checklist items** — Close and compliance tasks with blocking/non-blocking classification
- **Advisory notes** — Recommendations from the intelligence engine with supporting analysis

## Your team's daily interface

![](/uploads/illustrations/cuate/version-control.svg)

The Canvas Assistant is how your team interacts with Canvas day-to-day. It's not a chatbot — it's a work interface that understands accounting, knows your clients, and can act on your behalf with your approval.

Your team can ask questions, get answers grounded in real data, queue actions, and approve outcomes — all through conversation. No navigating between modules. No looking up numbers. Just ask.
