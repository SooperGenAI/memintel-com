## AR Health Monitor — 4 agents

![](/uploads/illustrations/cuate/entries.svg)

Your receivables are your lifeline. These four agents watch every customer relationship continuously.

**DSO Monitor** tracks how many days, on average, your customers take to pay. When DSO creeps above your threshold, you know before it becomes a cash flow problem.

**Overdue AR Alert** surfaces every invoice that's past due — by customer, by amount, by days overdue. No more discovering a six-month-old invoice buried in a spreadsheet.

**Credit Limit Breach** alerts you when a customer's outstanding balance exceeds their approved credit limit. Stop extending credit you didn't intend to extend.

**AR Concentration Risk** flags when a single customer represents too large a share of your total receivables. One slow payer shouldn't threaten your cash position.

*Sample alert: "Customer Sharma Enterprises has ₹18.4 lakhs overdue beyond 90 days — 34% of total AR. Credit limit was ₹8 lakhs."*

## AP Management Monitor — 3 agents

![](/uploads/illustrations/cuate/version-control.svg)

Your payables affect your cash position and your vendor relationships. These agents keep both in balance.

**DPO Monitor** tracks how long you're taking to pay vendors. Too slow and you damage relationships. Too fast and you're leaving cash on the table.

**Large AP Due Alert** tells you, every week, which large payments are coming due in the next 7–14 days. No surprises in your cash planning.

**Vendor Concentration Risk** flags when your payables are concentrated with a single vendor. Supply chain disruption or vendor failure hits harder when you're dependent on one source.

*Sample alert: "₹42 lakhs due to 3 vendors in the next 7 days. Vendor ABC accounts for 61% of total AP — concentration risk flagged."*

## Cash & Treasury Monitor — 2 agents

![](/uploads/illustrations/cuate/assets.svg)

Cash is the one metric every CFO watches every day. These two agents make sure nothing slips past.

**Cash Position Alert** monitors the total cash balance across all accounts against a minimum threshold. When cash drops below the threshold, the CFO is alerted immediately — not at the end of the month.

**Cash Burn Rate** tracks how fast cash is declining over a rolling window. When the burn rate suggests you'll hit a critical level within a defined horizon, you get a forward-looking alert — not a retrospective one.

*Sample alert: "Cash balance ₹12.3 lakhs — below ₹15 lakh threshold. Current burn rate: ₹2.1 lakhs/week. Estimated to reach ₹0 in 6 weeks."*

## GL Variance Monitor — 1 agent

![](/uploads/illustrations/cuate/static-website.svg)

Budget variance is simple in principle. In practice, nobody looks at it until month-end. This agent looks every day.

**Budget Variance Alert** compares actual GL account balances against the approved budget. When any account exceeds its budget by more than a configured threshold, the alert fires. Catch overspending while there's still time to act — not when you're preparing the board pack.

*Sample alert: "Marketing expenditure is ₹8.2 lakhs against a monthly budget of ₹5 lakhs — 64% over budget with 12 days remaining in the period."*

## GST Risk Monitor — 5 agents

![](/uploads/illustrations/cuate/version-control2.svg)

GST errors are silent and expensive. These five agents catch them before they become notices.

**GST Rate Anomaly** detects transactions where the GST rate applied doesn't match the expected rate for that transaction type. Wrong rates applied consistently become significant liabilities.

**GST Liability Spike** flags when your GST liability in a period is significantly higher than your historical average. Unusual spikes need investigation before the filing.

**ITC Anomaly** monitors your Input Tax Credit — when claimed ITC deviates significantly from historical patterns, the agent flags it for review.

**Missing GST Info** surfaces transactions where GSTIN is missing or incomplete on vendor invoices. Missing GSTINs mean ITC you can't claim.

**GST Classification Error** checks for transactions where the HSN/SAC code appears inconsistent with the nature of the transaction.

*Sample alert: "14 vendor invoices in October missing GSTIN — ₹3.8 lakhs in ITC at risk. 2 transactions flagged for potential rate misclassification."*

## Close Integrity Monitor — 3 agents

![](/uploads/illustrations/cuate/publish.svg)

Month-end close problems build over 25 days but are discovered in 3. These three agents check close readiness continuously so your team arrives at month-end with nothing to fix.

**Bank Reconciliation Status Monitor** identifies unmatched bank transactions. Flags items unreconciled for more than 5 days so your team resolves them before close.

**Suspense Account Aging Monitor** watches suspense and clearing accounts for aging items. Flags entries sitting uncleared for more than 15 days.

**Accrual Reversal Missing Monitor** tracks period-end accruals and checks whether reversals have been posted. Flags unreversed accruals that will distort the next period's results.

*Sample alert: "3 bank transactions unreconciled beyond 5 days. 2 suspense items aging 18 days. 1 unreversed accrual from prior period — close readiness: At Risk."*

## Custom Agents — anything your client needs

![](/uploads/illustrations/cuate/writing.svg)

Every business has monitoring needs that don't fit a standard template. Custom agents are how you handle them.

Describe the monitoring intent in plain English — exactly as you'd explain it to a colleague. Canvas compiles that into fully deterministic logic. A new agent is live within minutes.

**Examples built for clients:**
- "Alert when this distributor's monthly purchase volume drops more than 30% vs the previous 3-month average"
- "Flag any expense category where a single vendor accounts for more than 40% of spend in a month"
- "Alert when the ratio of credit notes to invoices exceeds 15% for any customer"

Our team works with you to design agents for your specific client needs. No coding required on your side.
