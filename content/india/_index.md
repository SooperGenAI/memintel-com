## Module coverage

---

![](/img/icons/material-symbols/200/rounded/auto_awesome_mosaic.svg)
### Bookkeeping & Monitoring

Daily bank reconciliation with AI-assisted matching. Transaction reclassification queue. Chart of accounts health monitoring. Bill approvals and document management. 24×7 monitoring agents watching every client's books.

---

![](/img/icons/material-symbols/200/rounded/auto_fix.svg)
### GST Compliance & ITC Intelligence

Complete GST lifecycle — GSTR-1 with 7-category invoice classification, GSTR-3B computation, GSTR-2B 4-pass intelligent matching, government-portal JSON export. Full ITC intelligence: Section 17(5) blocked credit detection, Rule 37 non-payment reversal, Rule 42 exempt apportionment, Section 49A utilisation order (IGST→CGST→SGST) enforced. Full audit trail on every ITC adjustment.

---

![](/img/icons/material-symbols/200/rounded/timer.svg)
### TDS Compliance

Deduction computation per payment against applicable section rates. Challan generation and deposit tracking. Form 26Q and 27Q quarterly return preparation. 26AS reconciliation to verify deductions match government records. NSDL-format text export.

---

![](/img/icons/material-symbols/200/rounded/design_services.svg)
### Month-End Close

Structured close process with automated validation. Trial balance verification. Suspense account clearance. Accrual reversal verification. GST/TDS cross-verification against filed returns. Close Readiness Score updated daily.

---

![](/img/icons/material-symbols/200/rounded/devices.svg)
### Continuous Monitoring

43 pre-built agents across 13 modules. AR health, AP management, cash and treasury, GL variance, close integrity, GST risk, India compliance. Custom monitoring rules in plain English for industry-specific requirements.

---

![](/img/icons/material-symbols/200/rounded/auto_awesome_mosaic.svg)
### Advisory & CFO Reports

Monthly CFO advisory report. Executive summary, compliance scorecard, ITC optimisation, vendor/customer risk, industry benchmarks. Proforma P&L, cash flow forecast, intelligence dashboard.
