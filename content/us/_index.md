## Module coverage

---

![](/img/icons/material-symbols/200/rounded/auto_awesome_mosaic.svg)
### Bookkeeping & Monitoring

Daily bank reconciliation with AI-assisted matching. Transaction reclassification queue. Chart of accounts health monitoring. Bill approvals and approval workflows. Document requests and client upload portal. 24×7 monitoring agents watching every client's books.

---

![](/img/icons/material-symbols/200/rounded/auto_fix.svg)
### Sales Tax & Nexus Tracking

Economic nexus monitoring across all 50 states. Revenue and transaction count tracked against each state's threshold. Approaching threshold alerts before nexus is triggered. Sales tax filing calendar with due dates, liabilities, and filing history. State registration management.

---

![](/img/icons/material-symbols/200/rounded/timer.svg)
### 1099 Compliance

Vendor payment aggregation across all payment types. 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC eligibility determination. W-9 collection tracking with automated follow-up emails. Compute Now engine recalculates eligibility on demand. Export to Track1099 CSV format.

---

![](/img/icons/material-symbols/200/rounded/performance_max.svg)
### Payroll Tax Oversight

Form 941 quarterly filing calendar with deposit variance detection. Form 940 annual FUTA tracking. State SUTA deadlines by jurisdiction. Payroll journal entry review with GL comparison and variance flagging. Suggested correcting entries.

Structured close process with automated validation gates. Book close checklist with blocking and non-blocking items. AR reconciliation with match rate scoring. AP reconciliation. Bank reconciliation. Close Readiness Score updated daily.

## Run your entire practice through conversation

![](/uploads/illustrations/cuate/static-website.svg)

The Canvas Assistant is how your team operates Canvas day-to-day. Ask it anything about any client — it pulls real data and gives you answers grounded in the books.

- *"What should I focus on today?"* → Prioritised briefing: sales tax deadline, overdue AR, missing W-9s
- *"Which states are approaching nexus threshold?"* → Multi-state nexus summary with days/revenue remaining
- *"What journal entries need my review?"* → Pending draft entries with source workflow and amounts
- *"Send a payment reminder to [client]"* → Composes email with invoice detail, waits for your send
- *"What's our 1099 penalty exposure if we don't collect W-9s?"* → Calculates backup withholding + filing penalties
- *"Are there any payroll tax deadlines this week?"* → Jurisdiction-level calendar summary
